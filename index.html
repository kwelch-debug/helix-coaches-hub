    Heat rules — what each WBGT reading means

    From the Helix Heat & Humidity Policy. WBGT is measured at every practice.

    WBGTWhat to do
    Under 82.0Normal activities. At least 3 rest breaks each hour, 3+ minutes each.
    82.0 – 86.9Use discretion for intense or prolonged exercise; watch at-risk players closely. At least 3 rest breaks each hour, 4+ minutes each.
    87.0 – 89.9Max practice 2 hours. Football: helmet, shoulder pads and shorts only; remove all protective equipment for conditioning. All sports: at least 4 rest breaks each hour, 4+ minutes each.
    90.0 – 92.0Max practice 1 hour. No protective equipment, no conditioning. 20 minutes of rest breaks within the hour.
    Over 92No outdoor workouts. Cancel or delay practice until a cooler reading.

    Academic Eligibility

    Requirements

    • Minimum 2.0 GPA, maintained throughout the season.
    • Enrolled in at least 12.5 quarter units of coursework.
    • Making minimum progress toward graduation requirements.
    • No more than one "U" in conduct/effort per grading period.
    • Must attend school all day to practice or play that day. See the handbook for excused exceptions.

    Source: Student-Athlete Handbook.

    Academic appeal process

    A 9th grade student gets a one-time academic probation for a GPA below 2.0 (on a 4.0 scale). The same student may also be allowed a separate, one-time academic probation after their 9th grade year.

    Appeals are not automatic. They are reviewed case by case. Academic progress, effort and input from teachers are factors in approving or denying an appeal. A student with two "U" grades can't use probation.

    How a student appeals

    1. Meet with each teacher who gave the "F." Discuss why the "F" was earned and what the student can do to improve the grade.
    2. Type a one-page letter with three paragraphs:
      • At the top: student's name, ID #, coach's name and sport.
      • Paragraph 1: why the student earned the "F."
      • Paragraph 2: what the student will do differently to improve the grade.
      • Paragraph 3: the interventions available (Tuesday/Wednesday tutorial, teacher appointments, library services, after-school tutorials, etc.).
    3. Give the letter to each teacher who issued the "F." If the contents are acceptable, the teacher signs and dates it.
    4. The student and a parent/guardian sign and date the letter.
    5. Submit the letter to the student's GLP (Grade Level Principal).

    The coach is notified of the student's eligibility status within three school days.

    Transfers

    How transfers work

    • Residential eligibility is set when a student first enrolls in 9th grade at a CIF school.
    • A transfer that isn't tied to a valid change of residence or a documented hardship (e.g., divorce, court order, school-safety issue) carries a sit-out period.
    • A student who transfers to follow a coach or move to a club coach must document that the move isn't athletically motivated.

    Full rules: CIF San Diego Section.

    What coaches must do

    Please provide the names of any transfer students to the Athletic Director to initiate the transfer process.

    Transfer students MAY NOT COMPETE in scrimmages or contests until cleared by CIF.
